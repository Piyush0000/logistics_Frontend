  • Motion: a soft light sweep crosses the band; sparklines draw left to right; partner rows cascade in.

Animations and micro-interactions (these apply to whichever direction you pick)

Try them: press the button, flip the switch, watch the loaders.

Click: copy AWB
  • Purpose first: motion only explains a change (something arrived, moved, saved, failed). Nothing loops forever except "live" indicators.
  • Timing: 120–180 ms for presses and hovers, 200–300 ms for panels and dialogs, up to 700 ms for chart entrances. One shared easing curve.
  • Page loads: menu and top bar stay put between pages; the content area shows a skeleton of its real shape, then each card streams in on its own (faster perceived speed).
  • Feedback: every button shows pressed → working (the "=" loader) → done/failed; saves confirm inline with a check and an undo toast where safe.
  • Numbers: KPIs count to their new value on refresh; status badges cross-fade when a shipment changes state.
  • Lists: new rows slide in, removed rows collapse; filters animate the result count; drag-to-reorder for courier priority.
  • Phone: bottom tab bar, sheets that slide up instead of dialogs, swipe actions on shipment rows, pull to refresh, light haptic on supported phones.
  • Respect settings: people with "reduce motion" on get instant changes, no movement.
